Our Story

More than coffee.
It's our culture.

Kaapi House was born from a simple desire — to share the taste of a perfect filter coffee the way our grandmothers made it. From hand-roasted beans to the iconic steel tumbler-davara, every detail honours South Indian tradition.

🫘

Farm-to-Cup Beans

Sourced from the Nilgiri hills — hand-selected Arabica and Robusta blends, roasted in-house every week.

🏺

Traditional Brewing

The old-school brass filter press. No machines, no shortcuts. Just gravity, patience, and time.

🍽️

Homemade Snacks

Murukku, bonda, vada, pongal — made fresh every morning by our kitchen team, just like amma makes.
